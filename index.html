About Me

Your trusted mortgage expert

I'm Marjan Alaeddini, a licensed Mortgage Agent Level 1 proudly serving the Greater Toronto Area. I spent over 15 years in Canadian banking, most of it around mortgages, before moving to this side of the table — helping families and individuals finance their future, from first-time buyers to investors who need alternative financing.

As a bilingual advisor fluent in both English and Farsi, I genuinely understand the unique needs of the Iranian-Canadian community. My mission: to make your mortgage journey smooth, transparent, and rewarding — in the language you're most comfortable with.
